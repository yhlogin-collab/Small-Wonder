Good to know

The details you'll want

What ages is it for?

The shows are geared for ages 3–10 — ideal for preschool through the early grades, and engaging for the whole room.

How big can the assembly be?

From a single class of 20–30 up to a full assembly of 500. We scale the show and the sound to fit.

What do you need from us?

Just the space and the students. We're fully self-contained — our own stage, sound, and lighting — and we handle setup and pack-up.

Do you do Chanukah & Purim programs?

Yes — holiday programming is one of our busiest and most-loved offerings. Book early for the season; those weeks fill quickly.

Is the content appropriate for our school?

Every script is reviewed by a Rav, and our shows are warmly received across the full spectrum of Jewish observance — and at many secular schools too.

Which show? For an assembly, our flagship Puppet Show is the go-to; the Ventriloquist Show is a great pick for a smaller, more intimate group — see both shows.
