A few of our favorites

A quick look at some of the shows families and camps ask for again and again. Video clips coming soon.

The Cow That Kept Shabbat

A heartwarming favorite: an adorable cow who refuses to work on Shabbos. Through charm, humor, and lovable animal characters, kids discover the beauty and holiness of Shabbos.

🎬 Video clip coming soon

The Two Brothers

Two brothers love each other so deeply that each secretly tries to help the other. Gentle, funny, and meaningful — a show about the beauty of caring for another Jew.

🎬 Video clip coming soon

The Unhappy King

A gloomy king who has everything — except happiness. With clowns, royal advisors, and plenty of laughs, kids learn that true happiness comes from helping others.

🎬 Video clip coming soon

The Garbage and the Kotel

An old man searches for the Kotel — only to find it hidden beneath years of garbage. With humor, suspense, and a powerful reveal, kids experience the thrill of uncovering the Kotel once again.

🎬 Video clip coming soon

The Monkey with the Gold Coins

A mischievous monkey, a mean Poritz, and a surprising pile of gold coins. Through laughter and suspense, kids learn the power of bitachon — that Hashem can help in the blink of an eye.
