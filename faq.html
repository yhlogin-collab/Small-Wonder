FAQ

Good questions, quick answers.

Everything you need to know before you book. Don't see your question? Call or text us at 1-805-738-8405 — we answer fast.

What's a Small Wonder show like?

A typical show runs about 40–45 minutes and blends two or three puppet shows with a lively musical program. It's highly interactive — the kids sing, laugh, and shout along — and every show carries a real lesson.

What ages is it best for?

The shows are geared for ages 3–10 — and they tend to charm the grown-ups in the room too.

How big can the audience be?

Anything from an intimate birthday party of 20–30 up to a crowd of 500. We scale and set up to fit the room you've got.

Do you travel to us? Which areas?

Yes — we're fully self-contained and come to you. We do most of our shows in the Tri-State area (New York, New Jersey & Connecticut) and the Dallas area, and we travel further afield depending on the season. Just ask.

What do you need from us?

Just the space and your audience. We bring our own stage, sound, and lighting, and we handle setup and pack-up ourselves.

How much space do we need?

Not much — a living room, a classroom, a corner of a hall, or a backyard. Our stage folds open in seconds and adapts to whatever room you have, indoors or out.

Puppet show or ventriloquist show — which should we pick?

The puppet show (about 40–45 minutes) is our interactive flagship and the crowd-pleaser. The ventriloquist show (about 30 minutes) is a great fit for a smaller, more intimate gathering. Not sure? Tell us your crowd and we'll help you choose.

Is the content appropriate?

Absolutely. Every script we perform is reviewed by a Rav, and our shows are warmly received across the full spectrum of Jewish observance — and at many secular schools too.

How far ahead should we book?

The sooner the better — especially for the busy Chanukah and summer-camp seasons. Send us your date and we'll hold your spot while we confirm the details.

Ready when you are
