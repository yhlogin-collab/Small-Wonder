Good to know

The camp-director details

How big a group can you handle?

Anywhere from a small group of 20–30 up to a full camp assembly of 500. We tune the show and the sound to the room.

What do you need from us?

Just the space and the campers. We bring our own stage, sound, and lighting, indoors or out, and we set up and pack up ourselves.

How long is the show?

About 40–45 minutes — two or three puppet shows plus a lively musical program, all highly interactive.

When should we book?

Summer fills up fast — the earlier the better. Send us your dates and we'll hold your spot while we work out the details.

Which show? For a big camp crowd, our flagship Puppet Show is the crowd-pleaser. For a smaller, more intimate group, the Ventriloquist Show is a great fit — see both shows.
