Good to know

The details parents ask about

How much space do we need?

Not much — a living room or a corner of the backyard is plenty. We set up a compact stage and adapt to whatever room you have.

What ages is it for?

It's built for kids and plays beautifully for a mixed-age crowd. Tell us the ages when you book and we'll tune the show to the room.

How long is the show?

The puppet show runs about 45 minutes; the ventriloquist show about 30. Both leave plenty of time for cake and the rest of the party.

Indoor or outdoor?

Either works. For outdoor parties we just ask for shade and a level spot — we'll talk through the setup when we confirm your date.

What do we need to provide?

Just the space and your guests. We bring the stage, sound, puppets, and performers, and we handle setup and cleanup.

Which show is right for a party — puppet or ventriloquist?

The puppet show is our crowd-pleasing flagship. The ventriloquist show is a great pick for a smaller, more intimate gathering. Not sure? Ask us and we'll help you choose.
